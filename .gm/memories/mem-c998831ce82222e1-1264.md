casey's inbound turn pipeline lives in src/hooks/. Admission (admission.js): the per-contact claim is taken SYNCHRONOUSLY, with no await between Set.has and Set.add; a burst is BUFFERED raw and replayed as full turns, and over BUFFER_CAP (20) the OLDEST drops; a rate-limited inbound sends no reply. Drop reasons are enumerated in dropped-intake.js DROP_REASONS. Identity: conversationKey is per CONTACT and replyTarget is the CHANNEL. Agent turn (turn-attempts.js): in-loop judge retries; ADVICE is always HELD on a spent budget (or replaced with a composed no-advice reply), while most other shapes retry then SEND ANYWAY. SYSTEM-PROMPT ECHO (8 or more words verbatim from the real prompt, fenced regions removed first) and TOOL-NAME LEAK (a literal case_* name) are code gates decided ABOVE the judge call; both retry then hold as the jargon leak does, never blank. Media is normalised (WhatsApp object vs Discord ARRAY); intake is APPEND-ONLY; a pin writes lat/lon with location_source:'gps'; the 24h WhatsApp window is measured from the contact's own LAST INBOUND. reply-judge.js adjudicates the reply against named fault shapes (each requires a verbatim quote; two passes must agree except the absence-only shapes), and evaluateCandidate routes each verdict.
