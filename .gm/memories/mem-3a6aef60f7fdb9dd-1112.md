casey design principles rationale. No mocks, fallbacks or stubs -- only singular mechanisms and loud errors. A degraded turn never fabricates case content, with two exceptions: a live first-attempt turn still degraded after its budget sends a truthful status, and a background re-drive stays silent. The LLM records the report, casey does no extraction, and a complete report is not a dead-end. Personal data goes only to processors casey can hold to a no-training policy (CASEY_LLM_DATA_POLICY default zdr; allow disables the guarantee). The bot never contacts anybody; everybody contacts the bot (CASEY_PROACTIVE_SENDS=off); there is ONE gate at the sending seams. A case is keyed per contact, not per channel. Areas/hand-over/day: a report in a mapped area goes to the primary ranger (else the first valid backup); autoAssignByArea runs once per OPEN UNASSIGNED record after a write touching area/location; resolution is EQUALITY via resolveArea with no gazetteer or fuzzy match, and an unmatched area goes to unmappedAreas. The ranger hands over and the technician signs off with a diagnosis plus resolution.
