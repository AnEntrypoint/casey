Dashboard stylesheet rules. The html root grounds off data-casey-theme with --paper and --ink, never --bg, because the kit's theme.js sets data-theme to auto and --bg then follows the device (a white root under a dark app). Keep the .ds-247420 prefix on rules touching btn-link, dropdown triggers, h2 and button sizes, and .app-chrome and .app-status, because kit scoped rules beat a bare class whatever the load order. Text contrast is 4.5:1: never --accent-ink or --sky on --bg-2, never a --danger fill as text, never opacity dimming; use --fg-2 or --fg-3, --danger-ink, --ink on --warn, and --green on the dark Done pill. Leaflet clusters and controls have fixed light backgrounds, so pin their text to --ink or themed text falls to about 2:1 in dark. The rail never gets overflow:hidden without an inner scroller (.ds-rail-stack scrolls, otherwise case detail is unreachable); the Back row is sticky on an opaque ground while the case header is not; the rail is docked, never floating over the map. On a phone, hide map and list with display:none and never unmount, so Leaflet keeps its viewport. .ds-map-shell's chrome layer keeps pointer-events:none or the map cannot be dragged. In the phone header span.crumb-right must claim the row, the track floor is 5.75rem, z-index sits above Leaflet's 400 to 1000, and the login banner is selected as the gate's sibling because the gate is fixed at --z-modal. Gutters collapse at the kit's 480px and phone rules come last for equal specificity. Urgency is encoded by size, ring and a short flag, never hue alone or a full-height stripe (stacked rows merge into one line); the :not(.active) and :not(.selected) guards stay; words and aria-label also carry it. The pulse is bounded to about 6 s (WCAG 2.2.2) and reduced-motion drops motion but keeps ring and size; each sheet guards its own transitions. The 44px touch floor must outrank the kit's .btn-sm and the icon-only nav toggle needs its padding reset or it collapses. Under forced-colors fields keep a zero-specificity outline so the kit focus ring still wins. td uses overflow-wrap:break-word plus min-width, never anywhere (it collapses min-content so scroll never engages), and not th. Flex items holding contact text take min-width:0 and wrap.
