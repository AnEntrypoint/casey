casey soft-delete, erasure and sqlite internals. Erasing a team member releases their work and unlinks their login. Cookie and password comparisons use crypto.timingSafeEqual. Soft deletes mean 'deleted' is a FIELD: t.list filters them, t.get does NOT, so the session middleware must check status as well as disabled and session epoch, and deleteAccount must bump session_epoch before removing the row. busybase opens with journal_mode=delete and busy_timeout=0, so sqlite NEVER waits -- it fails instantly with SQLITE_BUSY and a plain reader blocks a writer, so any concurrent reader (another CLI, a backup, an audit script) can fail casey's writes. src/store/busy-retry.js is the only defence: a USERLAND full-jitter retry inside a 5s budget that cannot save a write from a reader holding the lock continuously, with a spent intake budget COUNTED.
