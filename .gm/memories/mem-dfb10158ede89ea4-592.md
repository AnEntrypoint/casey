CASEY_CONFIG_DIR in casey holds thatcher.config.yml, report-fields.yml and persona.cjs; when it is absent casey falls back to bundled config/default/ plus the repo-root thatcher.config.yml. The three files do NOT share a resolver: report-fields.yml and persona.cjs come from CASEY_CONFIG_DIR or config/default/, but thatcher.config.yml is resolved by CaseStore/readThatcherFieldEnum from CASEY_CONFIG_DIR-or-process.cwd() and NOT from config/default/, so a new package must copy the root thatcher.config.yml alongside itself. CASEY_CONFIG_DIR wholesale-REPLACES the config, it does not merge.
